How to Bid on BIDSPACE

BIDSPACE makes online auctions simple, transparent, and fun. Follow these steps to start winning unique items from campus sellers and trusted collectors.

1. Create Your Free Account

Click Join in the top right. Use your college email for faster verification and access to exclusive student-only auctions. Registration takes less than a minute.

2. Browse & Discover

  • Explore categories: Art, Jewelry, Furniture, Collectibles, Fashion, and more.
  • Use the search bar to find specific items or sellers.
  • Check “Ending Soon” for last-chance opportunities.
  • Add items to your Watchlist (heart icon) so you never miss a closing auction.

3. Understand the Bidding Rules

  • Current Bid — The highest bid placed so far.
  • Minimum Increment — Usually $25 above the current bid (shown on the bid form).
  • Countdown Timer — Auctions end when the timer reaches zero. No late bids are accepted.
  • Live Updates — Bid history and current price update instantly when new bids arrive.

4. Place Your Bid

On any auction detail page:

  • Enter an amount equal to or higher than the minimum required.
  • Or click one of the quick-bid buttons for common increments.
  • Confirm — your bid is recorded immediately and appears in the bid history.

If someone outbids you, you’ll see the new price. You can bid again at any time before the auction ends.

5. Winning & Payment

  • If you are the highest bidder when the timer ends, you win!
  • You’ll receive a confirmation and payment instructions (campus pickup or local delivery).
  • Pay within 48 hours to secure the item. Failure to pay may result in account restrictions.

Tips for Successful Bidding

  • Set a personal maximum budget before you start and stick to it.
  • Watch the last 5–10 minutes of high-interest lots — activity often increases.
  • Read the full description and examine all photos carefully.
  • Check the seller’s other listings for more treasures.

Need Help?

Contact the BIDSPACE support team via the Help Center or email support@bidspace.college. We’re here to make sure every student has a great auction experience.
